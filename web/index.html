Reading the dip. The expected path never actually falls below today — it flattens through 2027, then resumes. The only real dip is the bear case (a trough around $930k, roughly −7%, late-27 to mid-28). The opportunity isn't betting on a crash; it's the soft patch in 2027 — similar prices to today, but stock sitting and vendors flexible. With $800k at 0%, waiting through that window costs you almost nothing.

!

Honest note on "live." This page can't embed an auto-refreshing feed — realestate.com.au and Domain don't permit it on a static site, and anyone claiming otherwise is scraping against their terms. The links above are the robust alternative: they always open current results. For a genuinely embedded feed that refreshes on a schedule, the only compliant route is the Domain Developer API pulled by a scheduled job — documented in the project README as the upgrade path.

How this model works. A four-layer hybrid: a city-level mean-reversion spine, a probability-weighted scenario engine (the fan chart above), a suburb-level relative-performance layer (the heat map), and a property-level hedonic adjustment for land and knock-down-rebuild potential. It is reproducible — the Python in /model regenerates these exact baseline numbers. Full methodology is in the project's skills folder.
General information only — not financial, legal or tax advice. All figures are approximations as at mid-2026 and will change. Suburb medians vary 10–20% between sources and are shown as ranges; no public source publishes median block size, so the 450sqm requirement must be confirmed per-listing via Landgate. Forecasts are scenario estimates conditioned on the stated assumptions and will be wrong if those assumptions are wrong. Verify against live REIWA, Landgate, RBA and ABS data and licensed professionals before acting.
